i-Ready Math

  • My role: Product manager, associate director of product management. I led the development of hundreds of interactive digital lessons, then built and managed a high-functioning team of 6 product managers to continue development.
  • Product Impact: i-Ready is an industry-leading K-8 personalized learning program used by 14+ million students across the US. It has demonstrated significant positive impacts on student achievement thanks to its conceptual strength, deep interactivity, rich feedback, and integration with the i-Ready Inform diagnostic assessment.

i-Ready Pro Math

  • My role: “Zero-to-one” product manager leading the creation of the next generation of Curriculum Associates’ personalized middle school math program, from product vision and roadmap to post-release analytics and iteration.
  • Product Impact: The program is brand-new in the 2025-26 school year, but it already has 1+ million users. Early data indicates a major (10%+) positive impact on student performance on critical math topics and significant improvements to student engagement, thanks to the program’s powerful and nuanced adaptivity; its balance between learning acceleration and foundational skills intervention.

Imagine Math PreK-2

  • My role: Project manager, instructional lead. I coordinated a 50+-person cross-functional team of teachers, curriculum developers, artists, animators, voice actors, programmers, and software testers. I built and managed the project’s schedule, coordinating management of risks and issues; I also served as one of two e-learning design leads on the project.
  • Product Impact: As Reasoning Mind Blueprint, the program achieved early recognition for combining conceptual strength with a joyful, whimsical approach to early childhood mathematics. It won the NewSchools Ignite Challenge and was ultimately acquired by Imagine Learning, where it has served as a core offering of Imagine Math, a personalized learning program used by 2+ million students annually.
