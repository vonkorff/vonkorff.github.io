Chatbot Tutor

  • What is it? I built a chatbot math tutor to experiment with ideas for improving generative AI's math tutoring ability.
  • Personalization via RAG: Generative AI tutors typically don't how the user was previously taught, so they often suggest "generic" solutions strategies. I leveraged RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) to make a chatbot tutor that uses solution strategies from the student's own textbook.
  • Agentic self-correction: Chatbot tutors often "hallucinate", making mathematical errors or simply providing confusing or misleading guidance to the user. Can we mitigate this problem by adding a second agent to monitor and advise the first?
  • Made with: 100% vibe-coded in Javascript (a language in which I have no proficiency), using OpenAI's Codex.
